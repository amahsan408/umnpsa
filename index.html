About Us

The Pakistani Students Association (PSA) is a registered student group at the University of Minnesota. We bring together Pakistani and Pakistani-American students — and anyone curious about our culture — to build friendships, share traditions, and support one another throughout college.

🎉

Culture

Celebrate Independence Day, Eid, Basant, and more with music, dance, and traditional dress.

🍛

Food

Biryani, chai, samosas, and jalebi — our events are a taste of home (and a great intro if you're new!).

🤝

Community

Meet students who share your background, find mentors, and make lifelong friends on campus.

🏏

Fun

Cricket matches, game nights, chai socials, and collaborations with other cultural groups.
